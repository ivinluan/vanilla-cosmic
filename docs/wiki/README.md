# Vanilla Cosmic Wiki

Welcome to the gameplay reference for Vanilla Cosmic. This wiki documents the mod’s current blocks, their operating inputs, outputs, transfer behavior, and defensive effects.

## Reading the tables

- **Input** means an ongoing operating resource or accepted material, not a construction or research requirement.
- **Output** means produced resources for crafters and extractors; for distribution and defense blocks, it describes their transport or protective effect.
- Sprite previews use existing mod artwork. Composite previews in the extractor and distribution pages are documentation-only images and do not replace gameplay sprites.

## Current scope

The wiki covers all 65 current Cosmic Vanilla HJSON block definitions. Construction, research, durability, and balance rationale remain in the project’s [guides](../guides/).
