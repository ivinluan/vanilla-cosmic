# Progress Report

## October 2026

- Updated Vanilla Cosmic to v3.10.28 for [Mindustry v160.7](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/tree/v160.7).
- Refined block descriptions and rebalanced progression, durability, and construction costs.
- Expanded extraction with cosmic drills, bores, and fixed-output Rift Fracturers.
- Added cosmic production, item distribution, liquid transport, and Event Horizon ShieldWalls.
- Separated the Cosmic tech tree, enabled Cosmic blocks on Serpulo and Erekir, and maintained English and Filipino localization.
- Added balance guides and the project wiki.

## September 2024

- Began Vanilla Cosmic development, initially targeting [Mindustry v146](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/tree/v146).
- Released the mod through the Mindustry Mod Browser.
- Established the initial drill, mini-drill, bore, burst-drill, WallCrafter, and conveyor lineup.
- Added English and Filipino localization.
